# ADR-005: CNN-vs-ViT helmet-classifier outcome and runtime adoption posture

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-08-31
- **Decision owner:** TrafficPulse project lead (maintainer role)
- **Supersedes:** none
- **References:** `TRAFFICPULSE_MASTER_SPEC.md` §4; `docs/architecture-review.md` §12;
  `docs/evaluation-protocol.md` §8; `docs/phase-4-plan.md` P4-U5;
  [`docs/cnn-vs-vit-results.md`](../cnn-vs-vit-results.md);
  `experiments/helmet_cnn_vit/PREREGISTRATION.md` (git tag `p4u5-prereg`)

## Context

Master spec §4 requires a controlled CNN-versus-ViT comparison as the project's genuine
Vision Transformer contribution. `architecture-review.md` §12 locks it to helmet-state
classification with **ResNet-50 vs DeiT-Small**. Until P4-U5 this was the largest
outstanding gap against the specification: the only "ViT" in the runtime was
`openai/clip-vit-base-patch32`, a zero-shot CLIP classifier whose backbone happens to be a
ViT — which is **not** the mandated experiment and was never presented as one.

P4-U5 executed the experiment on the HELMET dataset (CC-BY-4.0, licence gate resolved)
under a pre-registration that was committed and git-tagged before the final seed runs. The
decision rule was frozen **in code** (`helmet_cnn_vit.stats.decide`) before any test-split
number existed.

Two facts now need recording, and they point in different directions:

1. **ResNet-50 won the accuracy comparison** under the frozen rule — mean test macro-F1
   0.92881 vs 0.91975, Δ = −0.00906, pooled 95% CI [−0.01380, −0.00434], sign-consistent
   across all three seeds.
2. **DeiT-Small is the cheaper model** on the measured RTX 4060 Laptop benchmark — fewer
   parameters, smaller checkpoint, and lower latency and VRAM at both batch sizes (−29.8%
   latency and −34.2% VRAM at batch 32).

A third fact governs what may be done with either: **the experiment trained and evaluated
on whole-motorcycle crops**, which is HELMET's native annotation granularity, while the
TrafficPulse runtime classifies **derived head crops**. The pre-registration states this
plainly: the winner is *measured*, not *deployed*.

## Decision drivers

- The pre-registered rule must be honoured exactly, in whichever direction it points
  (architecture-review §12; evaluation-protocol §8).
- No accuracy claim outside the experiment's own reported protocol (phase-4-plan P4-U5
  explicit exclusions).
- The evidence standard: a model is adopted on measured evidence for the input it will
  actually receive, not on a proxy.
- Optional-extra discipline — no ML dependency in the base install, no weights committed.

## Considered options

- **Record the outcome; adopt no runtime backend from it (chosen).** The experiment
  discharges the §4 obligation and the comparison is reported in full; the runtime helmet
  path is unchanged, and adoption is gated on a head-crop re-validation.
- **Adopt ResNet-50 as the runtime `HelmetClassifier` backend** — rejected: it won on
  motorcycle crops, which is not the input the runtime supplies. Shipping it would assert
  a validated accuracy the project does not have, and would breach the P4-U5 exclusion
  against accuracy claims outside the experiment's protocol.
- **Adopt DeiT-Small on the cost argument** — rejected for the same reason, and
  additionally because the frozen rule named the *other* model on the metric the protocol
  designated primary. Choosing the loser on a secondary axis, immediately after a
  pre-registered comparison, is exactly the post-hoc reasoning pre-registration exists to
  prevent.
- **Re-run the comparison on derived head crops before deciding** — deferred, not
  rejected. It is the right next experiment, but it is a new unit with its own
  pre-registration, not an amendment to this one.

## Decision

1. **The pre-registered outcome is recorded as-is: ResNet-50 is the winner of the accuracy
   comparison.** Both frozen conditions held (sign-consistency across three seeds; pooled
   95% CI excluding zero). The verdict is reported as the rule produced it — the ~0.9
   macro-F1 point margin is stated, not characterised as decisive or negligible, because
   the rule carries no minimum-effect threshold.
2. **The cost trade-off is recorded with equal weight and not resolved.** DeiT-Small is
   smaller and more efficient on every measured axis. The experiment does not adjudicate
   accuracy against cost, and this ADR does not either.
3. **No `HelmetClassifier` backend is adopted from this experiment.** The runtime helmet
   path is unchanged: the zero-shot CLIP backend and the scripted stub remain the only
   implementations behind the P4-U4 seam. Neither experimental checkpoint is wired in, and
   no weights are committed.
4. **Adoption is gated** on a subsequent, separately pre-registered evaluation of the
   candidate on **derived head crops** — the input the runtime actually produces — plus a
   calibration check on that same input distribution.
5. **The observed anomalies are documented, not corrected.** Boundary-valued LR selections,
   the seed-0 McNemar p = 0.0531, the 29-crop height-bucket prose/code mismatch, the
   random-init benchmark timing, the unmeasured `motion_blur` severity-3 cell, and the
   absence of bitwise training determinism are all recorded in
   [`docs/cnn-vs-vit-results.md`](../cnn-vs-vit-results.md) §9. None was repaired
   retrospectively.

## Consequences

- **Master spec §4 is discharged.** The mandatory CNN-vs-ViT comparison has been executed,
  is reproducible from committed configs and seeds, and is reported with its statistics,
  calibration, robustness slices, cost benchmark, and limitations. `ENGINEERING_HISTORY.md`
  §9.2 no longer names it as the largest gap.
- **The runtime is unchanged by this unit**, so no regression surface is introduced: no new
  dependency, no new optional extra, no change to the P4-U4 seam or to any reasoning path.
- **The project may cite the comparison** but may not cite it as a deployed-model accuracy
  figure. `docs/cnn-vs-vit-results.md` §11 fixes the boundary between what may and may not
  be claimed, and that section governs.
- **A follow-up unit is implied**: head-crop re-validation before any real helmet
  classifier ships. Until then the no-helmet reasoning path continues to run on the
  zero-shot backend, whose own limitations are recorded separately.
- **The `motion_blur` severity-3 defect remains open** in `experiments/helmet_cnn_vit/`.
  Repairing it requires amending and re-freezing the corruption spec, and re-running that
  pass; it is deliberately out of scope here.

## Risks and mitigations

- **Risk:** the result is read as "CNNs beat ViTs", a claim far broader than the evidence.
  *Mitigation:* `cnn-vs-vit-results.md` §11 states the permitted claim explicitly, and §9.1
  records that both LR selections sat on grid boundaries, so neither family is demonstrated
  to have been tuned to its own optimum.
- **Risk:** the cost advantage is used to justify shipping DeiT-Small anyway.
  *Mitigation:* decision 3 forbids adopting either model without head-crop validation; the
  gate is on the *input distribution*, so it binds both candidates equally.
- **Risk:** the small margin is later reported as a decisive architectural finding.
  *Mitigation:* the margin, the CI, and the non-significant seed-0 McNemar are all recorded
  next to the headline rather than in an appendix.

## Revisit triggers

- A head-crop re-validation of either candidate completing.
- Any proposal to ship a trained `HelmetClassifier` backend behind the P4-U4 seam.
- An amended, re-frozen corruption spec that allows the `motion_blur` severity-3 cell to be
  measured.
- Acquisition of licence-cleared Indian footage, which would change the transfer question
  the experiment explicitly does not address.
